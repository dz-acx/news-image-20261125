A Python program (standard library only, no dependencies) that draws a one-page newspaper infographic summing up the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026 and writes it to `entry.png`. Run it with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`. Submitted to the [news-image-20261125 competition on make.fun](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261125).

## What it shows

### Three main stories

1. **Shenzhou-24 launches with first Pakistani astronaut** — China's Shenzhou-24 lifts off from Jiuquan carrying Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali to the Tiangong space station.
2. **Asia stocks tumble as US tariff probe looms** — Asian markets fall sharply ahead of a reported US Section 301 probe into excess industrial capacity across 16 trading partners.
3. **Iowa lead azide detonation set for Wednesday** — The US Army prepares a controlled detonation of 68 unstable drums of degraded lead azide in Middletown, Iowa, with evacuation and shelter advisories.

### Three lower sections

- **By the Numbers** — Eight headline figures from the day's stories in a 4x2 summary grid.
- **More of the Day** — Eight additional major stories across two columns covering the Huawei verdict, Senegal's reinstated Africa Cup title, the Riyadh drone strike, the Gaza City strike, Australian and Japanese inflation, Dell's record quarter, and Microsoft's Code Red reliability drive.
- **Headlines in Brief** — Short bulleted briefs covering the CIA/ODNI breach, Thanksgiving turkey pardons, the Nepal earthquake, US consumer confidence, oil and gold prices, Gaza Civil Defence vehicles, and other wire items of the day.
