# News of Wednesday 25 November 2026

## Shenzhou-24 launches with first Pakistani astronaut

China's Shenzhou-24 launched at 08:18 Beijing time on Wednesday from Jiuquan on a Long March-2F rocket. The crew of Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Pakistani pilot Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40, makes Ali the first foreign astronaut to visit the Tiangong space station; he stays 5 to 10 days as payload specialist, running a photosynthetic cyanobacteria experiment.

## Asia stocks tumble as US tariff probe looms

Asian shares fell sharply on Wednesday morning, with the MSCI Asia-Pacific index outside Japan down 2.3%, South Korean shares down as much as 5.3%, Taiwan down 5.1% and the Nikkei down 2.9%, after reports that the US is preparing to release a Section 301 probe into excess industrial capacity in 16 major trading partners, including China and the EU, paving the way for new tariffs.

## Iowa lead azide detonation set for Wednesday

The US Army scheduled a controlled detonation at about 9 a.m. CST on Wednesday at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown: about 9,500 pounds of degraded lead azide in 68 drums, too unstable to move. Residents within 5 miles were advised to evacuate and those 5 to 20 miles away to stay indoors, with a lead-dust plume possibly travelling 100 miles into northern Missouri.

## Huawei convicted in US trial

A Brooklyn federal jury on Tuesday convicted Huawei on 10 of 12 counts, including bank fraud, wire fraud, sanctions violations and conspiring to steal trade secrets, and acquitted it of racketeering conspiracy and obstructing justice. Sentencing is set for 14 September 2027 and Huawei plans to appeal.

## Senegal reinstated as Africa Cup champions

The Court of Arbitration for Sport on Tuesday upheld Senegal's appeal and restored their Africa Cup of Nations title, confirming the 1-0 extra-time final win over Morocco in Rabat; Morocco says it will challenge the ruling.

## Houthi drone hits Riyadh tower

A Houthi drone struck the Public Investment Fund Tower in Riyadh on Tuesday for the second time in less than a week, wounding 11 people: two Saudis, four Egyptians, three Nepalis and two Indians, Saudi Civil Defence said.

## Israeli strike kills Hamas security chief

An Israeli airstrike in western Gaza City on Tuesday killed Naeem Abu Nahel, director of Hamas's internal security apparatus; he was one of seven Palestinians killed, including two police officers, medics and police said.

## Australia inflation jumps

Consumer prices rose 4.9% in the year to October, up from 4.3%, with trimmed-mean inflation at 3.8%; the Reserve Bank has already raised its cash rate 125 basis points this year to 4.85% and meets again on 7-8 December.

## Japan services inflation at 36-year high

Japan's services producer price index rose 4.2% in October from a year earlier, the fastest pace since 1990, driven by ocean freight, leasing and hotel charges, bolstering the case for a Bank of Japan rate hike.

## Dell posts record quarter

Dell raised its annual forecasts for the third time after record third-quarter revenue of $54.94 billion; it now expects $202 billion of revenue for fiscal 2027, with a record $132.3 billion AI server backlog and more than 7,500 AI Factory customers.

## Microsoft declares Code Red

Internal memos reported by Reuters describe a Code Red overhaul after the 24-hour outage of 12-13 November that hit Exchange Online, Outlook, Teams and Copilot; Microsoft targets worldwide resolution within an hour in future incidents.
